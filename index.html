Camping, made less complicated

A few principles for building a kit without overspending.

Start with the trip, not the shopping cart

Consider climate, campground rules, trip length, transportation, and whether you can borrow gear before buying.

Prioritize the essentials

Safety, shelter, sleep, water, food, and weather-appropriate clothing come before extras.

Compare total cost

Check shipping, warranty, return terms, and replacement parts—not just the headline price.

Try before you commit

When possible, borrow or rent unfamiliar gear and test it at home before a trip.
