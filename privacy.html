Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information may be involved when you visit FRONTERA. FRONTERA is currently an outdoor product-discovery and editorial website. We do not currently process customer payments, fulfill orders, or offer visitor accounts on this site.

In short: You can browse our guides without creating an account. If you use the expedition planner, the destination name and trip dates you enter are sent to a third-party weather service to request a forecast. If you follow an affiliate link, the partner network or retailer may use tracking technologies to attribute a referral or purchase.

1. Information you provide

If you email FRONTERA, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any details you provide about a website issue or partnership inquiry. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information.

The expedition planner asks for a destination, dates, trip duration, group size, terrain, and activities so it can prepare a planning checklist and request available forecast information. Use a general destination such as a town, park, or region rather than a home address or other sensitive location.

2. Third-party services

Some parts of this website rely on services operated by other organizations. Those services may receive technical information from your browser, such as an IP address, device or browser details, and the page or resource being requested, in accordance with their own practices.

  • Website hosting: The website is published using GitHub Pages. Requests to the site are handled by GitHub and its infrastructure.
  • Weather forecasts: When you submit the expedition planner, the destination text and selected trip dates are sent to Visual Crossing to request forecast data. FRONTERA does not need your precise home address for this feature.
  • Fonts and images: The site may load fonts and photographs from third-party providers, including Google Fonts and image hosts. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser loads those resources.
  • Affiliate links: Some outbound product links may use affiliate tracking. Affiliate networks and retailers may use cookies or similar technologies to recognize a referral and attribute qualifying purchases. Their own privacy policies and terms govern those activities.

FRONTERA does not control the privacy practices of these third parties. Review their policies directly for details about their collection, use, retention, and choices.

3. Affiliate relationships

FRONTERA may earn a commission when you follow a qualifying affiliate link and make a purchase. The retailer or brand operates its own store and handles checkout, payment, shipping, returns, refunds, and warranties. When you leave FRONTERA, information you provide is subject to that seller's policies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

FRONTERA does not currently provide visitor accounts or a checkout system. Third-party services embedded in the site, affiliate networks, and retailers may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security, or referral attribution. Your browser settings may allow you to manage or block cookies, although some third-party features or referral tracking may not work as intended.

5. How information is used

Information you send directly to FRONTERA may be used to respond to your message, investigate a reported website issue, or discuss a potential brand relationship. Information entered into the expedition planner is used to request a forecast and create an on-screen planning result. We do not currently operate a FRONTERA customer account database or process purchases on this website.

6. Data retention and security

Email correspondence may remain in the email service used to receive and manage it. Third-party providers determine how long they retain technical or referral information under their own policies. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive information by email or through the planner.

7. Children's privacy

FRONTERA is intended for a general outdoor audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices and questions

You can choose not to use the expedition planner, not to follow affiliate links, and not to email personal information. You can also use browser controls to manage cookies and third-party content. For privacy questions or requests relating to information you sent directly to FRONTERA, email frontera.partnerships@gmail.com.

9. Updates to this policy

As FRONTERA adds features, partner integrations, and services, this policy may change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when the page was last revised. We encourage visitors to review it periodically.

Important: This page is a plain-language description of the website as it is currently configured, not legal advice. Before launching broader analytics, mailing lists, user accounts, or additional commercial features, FRONTERA should review and update this policy with qualified legal guidance for the jurisdictions it serves.
