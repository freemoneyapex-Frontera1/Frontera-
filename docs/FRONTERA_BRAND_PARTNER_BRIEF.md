# FRONTERA | Brand Partnership Brief
Updated: November 25, 2026

## Who we are
FRONTERA is an Arizona-based outdoor discovery and commerce platform being built to help people find their next adventure and the equipment that supports it. We bring outdoor gear discovery, practical planning, camp cooking, and field knowledge together in one accessible destination.

## Our vision
Make getting outside easier to start and more rewarding to pursue. FRONTERA is designed to welcome a first-time camper while still serving experienced hikers, overlanders, and outdoor cooks. We want to become a trusted starting point for outdoor discovery—not a wall of ads or an indiscriminate product dump.

## How partnership works
Our initial model is publisher-led affiliate commerce:
- Visitors discover relevant products and useful information on FRONTERA.
- Product pages and guides explain practical use cases and help visitors compare options.
- Customers complete purchases directly with the brand or authorized retailer.
- Where an approved affiliate program is available, FRONTERA uses the merchant's authorized tracking links and earns commission on qualifying sales.

FRONTERA does not currently process customer checkout, hold inventory, or handle shipping and returns.

## What we can offer partners
- Contextual placement in relevant outdoor categories and buying guides.
- Product discovery alongside trip planning and field-kitchen content.
- Real-world outdoor storytelling through the related Big Cherry Boys channel, where relevant and subject to product access and editorial fit.
- A brand-safe approach: accurate product details, approved creative assets, clear disclosures, and no unsupported testing or performance claims.
- A long-term relationship mindset focused on useful discovery and qualified referrals rather than short-lived promotion.

## Editorial and merchandising standards
- Relevance and visitor value come before commission rate.
- We do not claim to have tested gear unless we have actually used it.
- We use brand-approved images, descriptions, and product feeds according to partner terms.
- We clearly disclose affiliate relationships.
- We do not publish unverified prices, stock status, specifications, or warranty claims.
- We send customers to the brand or authorized retailer for checkout and post-purchase service.

## Current stage and honest expectations
FRONTERA is an early-stage platform. We are building the catalog, original content, and distribution deliberately. We do not represent unverified traffic, sales history, audience size, or conversion rates as established performance. We welcome the opportunity to demonstrate the platform, explain our content plan, and grow a measurable partnership over time.

## Partnership requests
We would value guidance on:
1. Publisher application and approval requirements.
2. Product data feeds, deep-linking tools, and approved image/creative assets.
3. Brand guidelines, restricted terms, and category exclusions.
4. Opportunities for product education, samples, or field-content collaboration when appropriate.
5. The best partner contact for ongoing communication.
