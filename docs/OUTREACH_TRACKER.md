## Follow-up operating rule

- Do not send repeated messages within a few days of the first inquiry.
- Review saved drafts on November 30, 2026, and send only if the partner has not replied in the meantime.
- Personalize each follow-up; do not mass-send identical messages.
- Pause outreach when a brand says it is not accepting the relevant business model.
- Record the date, recipient, response, program route, requirements, and next action after every meaningful exchange.

## Commercial guardrails

- Do not describe an inquiry as an approval or partnership.
- Do not publish a product until the merchant relationship, authorized tracking link, product data, image rights, and destination have been verified.
- Do not represent traffic, revenue, conversion, inventory, or product testing that FRONTERA cannot substantiate.
- Prioritize affiliate/referral and approved dropship routes that do not require FRONTERA to buy and hold stock.
