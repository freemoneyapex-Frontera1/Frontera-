This is an internal sourcing and approval queue, not a public product catalog. Nothing in this file is a live FRONTERA affiliate link or a claim that a brand has approved FRONTERA.

## Publishing gate

A product may enter `data/products.json` only after all of the following are true:

1. FRONTERA has an active approved affiliate relationship with the merchant, or written permission for a clearly disclosed non-affiliate listing.
2. The merchant or network supplies an approved tracking URL for the exact product or an authorized deep-linking method.
3. The exact product, destination URL, product description, image-use rights, and any displayed price/availability are verified.
4. The product record passes `data/product.schema.json` and the quality checks in this folder's product feed guide.

Until then, the public catalog remains empty. Do not substitute ordinary retailer URLs for affiliate links or publish illustrative records.

## Editorial rule

FRONTERA should not rank a product higher merely because its commission is higher. Choose products for fit, utility, reliability, clear specifications, and value to the visitor. Label sponsored placement clearly. Disclose affiliate relationships clearly. Never imply FRONTERA personally tested a product unless it actually has.
