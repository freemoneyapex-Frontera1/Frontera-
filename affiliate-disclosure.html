Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: November 26, 2026

FRONTERA believes outdoor recommendations should be useful, honest, and clear about how this website is supported.

Our disclosure: FRONTERA may earn a commission when you click certain links on this website and make a qualifying purchase from a participating brand or retailer. This generally does not increase the price you pay. Not every link is an affiliate link.

How it works

Some product links may be tracked through an affiliate network or directly by a participating merchant. If a qualifying purchase is attributed to FRONTERA, we may receive a commission. The brand or retailer—not FRONTERA—processes the order, payment, shipping, returns, refunds, and warranty service.

Our editorial approach

  • We aim to recommend products for their relevance and usefulness, not simply because a commission is available.
  • Affiliate relationships do not guarantee a positive review, a product placement, or a recommendation.
  • We do not claim to have personally tested equipment unless we have actually used it and clearly say so.
  • Product details, prices, stock, shipping, and availability can change. Confirm current information with the seller before buying.
  • Any paid sponsorship or other material commercial relationship will be identified clearly.

Your purchase

When you follow a product link, you leave FRONTERA and visit the seller's website. The seller's terms, privacy policy, product information, and customer-service policies apply to your purchase. FRONTERA does not currently operate a checkout or sell or ship products directly through this website.

Questions

Questions about a link or our partner relationships? Contact frontera.partnerships@gmail.com.

This disclosure is general website information, not legal advice. FRONTERA will review it as its affiliate relationships and commercial features develop.
